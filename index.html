Our Event!

Our event would have been held on Monday, March 16, 2020! Due to our school closing on March 13, 2020, our event got cancelled as well. We were very sad to have to inform the City Council members, including our mayor, school dignitaries, and many other representatives from the Arbor Day Foundation and the Arkansas Forestry Division that were planning to attend that it was canceled. We had puchased an autumn blaze from our local tree nursery to be planted on our campus, and our event would've included watching the planting and then a reception with cupcakes and drinks. The picture above is a greenhouse at the local nursery that we purchased our tree from. For more pictures of our tree, see the "Gallery" page!

We planned this event for weeks, so if you have any questions on how to start an event, please feel free to reach out to us. We are by far no experts, and we had a lot of help boucning around ideas and receiving contacts from our EAST facilitator and the Arkansas Forestry Commission. However, we would love to help in any way we can.

We are all in this together to make a Greener Today!!!
